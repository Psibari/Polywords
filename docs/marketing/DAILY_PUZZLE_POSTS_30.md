# 30 POLYWORDS Daily Puzzle Posts

Use these as lightweight prelaunch posts. Public posts should ask for guesses. The answer key is for you, not the first post.

Recommended caption pattern:

> WORD: [WORD]
> Which clues are REAL meanings, and which are TRAPS?
> In POLYWORDS, you swipe UP for REAL and RIGHT for TRAP.
> Would this feel fair, too easy, or annoying?
