# POLYWORDS First Flock Prelaunch Setup

## Purpose

POLYWORDS is not in full launch mode yet. This plan is for the stage before the app is ready: prove that strangers enjoy the core puzzle, collect early testers, and build a small waitlist without pretending the game is finished.

This is a **public-first plan**. Do not DM or text personal contacts unless someone already asks about the game. No contact scraping. No awkward friend spam. Let the puzzle pull people in.

## North-star goal

Get 100 people to say: **tell me when I can play this.**

That means email/waitlist signups, not likes. Likes are smoke. Waitlist names are kindling.

## Positioning

**POLYWORDS** is a mobile swipe word puzzle game where one word can have multiple real meanings. Polly, the reigning Master of Words, hides those meanings behind clever masks and traps. Swipe UP for real meanings. Swipe RIGHT for traps. Take the meanings back.

## Public hook

Use this everywhere:

> Can you spot the fake meaning?

Do not lead with "my app is coming soon." Lead with a puzzle people can play in three seconds.

## Waitlist name

Use: **The First Flock**

Button copy:

> Join the First Flock

Small support copy:

> Get early puzzle previews, beta access when ready, and a chance to help shape POLYWORDS before launch.

## Landing page structure

Use Carrd, Framer, Substack, Beehiiv, or a simple Google Form first. Do not overbuild the page.

### Hero

**POLYWORDS**

**Words have meaning...sss.**

Polly stole the meanings. Take them back.

### One-sentence explainer

POLYWORDS is a swipe-based mobile word puzzle game where one word has many real meanings, and Polly hides them behind close-but-wrong traps.

### How it works

- One target word appears.
- A stack of clue tiles appears.
- Swipe UP if the clue points to a real meaning.
- Swipe RIGHT if it is a trap.
- Survive the Hunt and take the word back from Polly.

### Sample puzzle

**WORD: BANK**

REAL or TRAP?

- river's muddy shoulder
- paychecks go quiet there
- airplane's side lean
- money sleeps in drawers

## Google Form setup

Title:

**POLYWORDS First Flock Waitlist**

Description:

> Join the first group to test POLYWORDS, a swipe-based word puzzle game about words with multiple meanings. You will get early puzzle previews, beta updates, and a chance to help shape the game before launch.

Fields:

1. Name
2. Email
3. Are you interested in beta testing? Yes / No / Maybe later
4. What device do you use? iPhone / Android / Both
5. Which best describes you? Puzzle fan / Word-game fan / Teacher / Tutor / Parent / Game dev / Other
6. Want daily sample puzzles before launch? Yes / No
7. Optional: What kind of word games do you already play?

## First posting strategy

Post one mini puzzle per day. The goal is comments and feedback, not promotion.

Best post structure:

1. Target word
2. Four or five clue tiles
3. Ask: which are REAL and which are TRAPS?
4. Mention the swipe mechanic in one sentence
5. Invite feedback: fair, too easy, or annoying?
6. Link to waitlist only after the puzzle has a reason to exist

## Audience order

Start with public and semi-public places where people already like puzzles, words, teaching, or indie games.

1. Personal public social posts, if comfortable
2. Indie game feedback spaces
3. Word game / puzzle communities
4. Teacher, tutor, homeschool, and ESL groups where feedback posts are allowed
5. Reddit feedback posts, not promotion posts
6. Product directories or beta directories once a landing page/demo exists
7. Short-form video posts built around one playable puzzle at a time

## No-DM rule

Do not DM or text people from Contacts as a growth tactic.

Allowed:

- replying when someone comments or asks about POLYWORDS
- sharing the waitlist link when someone requests it
- posting publicly in your own feed
- posting in groups only when the rules allow feedback or project posts
- inviting people through an opt-in form

Avoid:

- cold texting friends/family
- scraping contacts
- sending bulk Gmail outreach
- pretending a promo post is a personal message

## Reddit posture

Do not post like an advertiser. Post like a builder asking for a fairness check.

Good:

> I am building a word puzzle game around multiple-meaning words. Can you tell me if this puzzle feels fair or annoying?

Bad:

> Download my upcoming mobile game.

## Weekly target

- Week 1: landing page/form live, first 10 puzzle posts written, first public post live
- Week 2: post 1 puzzle/day, ask for feedback in 3 allowed public/semi-public communities, collect first 10-25 waitlist names
- Week 3: run Polly's 7-Day Meaning Challenge, collect 25-50 total names
- Week 4: invite first tester batch from people who opted in, collect feedback, improve the vertical slice

## Success metrics

Track these weekly:

- Waitlist signups
- People who ask to play
- Comments per puzzle
- Confusing clues flagged
- Teachers/tutors interested through opt-in comments/forms
- Number of testers ready for iOS/Android

## Do not do yet

- Do not spend money on ads.
- Do not chase influencers.
- Do not announce a launch date.
- Do not overbuild a Discord.
- Do not sell the full app before the vertical slice feels premium.
- Do not hide the idea until launch day.
- Do not DM/text personal contacts as a marketing tactic.
