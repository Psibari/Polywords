# POLYWORDS Content Philosophy

Governs the *feeling* behind every mask and trap. For mechanical rules (tile format, trap
styles, audit gates), see `docs/CONTENT_WRITING_STANDARD.md`.

## The Feeling We Are Creating

POLYWORDS is not a vocabulary game. It is a recognition game. Players already know these
meanings — they just don't realize how often their brain switches between them.

Goal is never "I learned a new definition." Goal is "I can't believe I never noticed that."

## What the Player Should Feel

Every REAL: **Hesitation** ("Wait, what is this talking about?") → **Recognition** ("Of
course, that's what this means.") → **Satisfaction** ("I knew that the whole time.")

The player should never feel tricked. They should feel like their own memory woke up.

## The Brain We Are Designing For

The brain stores moments and memories, not dictionary definitions. Nobody hears "match" and
thinks "a contest between opposing sides" — they think of a soccer match, a campfire, matching
socks. Write from those places.

## Every Meaning Lives Somewhere

Before writing, ask: where does this meaning live in a person's life — not the dictionary, real
life? BANK lives in paychecks, ATMs, mortgages, riverbanks. MATCH lives in campfires, sports,
dating, matching clothes. ROOT lives in gardens, family history, root beer floats.

## Recognition, Not Definition

A REAL is a memory trigger. A trap is the wrong memory trigger. The player already owns both
memories — the game asks them to choose the correct one.

## REALS

Should activate a familiar memory, feel natural, create 1-3 seconds of productive hesitation.
Never forced, never a dictionary, never the writer showing off. Success = "Oh... right."

## TRAPS

Not random — a trap activates the wrong memory first, close enough that an experienced speaker
hesitates before realizing it belongs to a neighboring meaning. A trap must lose honestly, never
because it's ridiculous.

## Every Word Has Its Own Voice

No universal writing style. Meanings live in sports, work, school, childhood, internet culture.
Write from where people actually encounter that meaning — don't force every word through the
same creative formula.

## The North Star

We are not teaching new words. We are revealing the hidden network of meanings people have been
using their whole lives without noticing. Every REAL unlocks the correct memory; every trap
tempts the wrong one. Success feels like recognition, not education.
