# POLYWORDS Content Writing Standard

## Authority

This document is the source of truth for meaning inventories, REAL masks, traps, hidden
meanings, and editorial approval.

It consolidates the approved guidance from:

1. `WORDwriting.docx` as the primary writing authority;
2. `GOLDEN RULE.docx` as the legality and fairness checklist;
3. `maskewriterGPT.docx` only where it does not conflict with the first two.

## Core Standard

POLYWORDS is not a vocabulary quiz. The player already knows the hero word and recognizes its
meanings through compressed human context.

The target reaction is:

> Wait...
>
> Oh. Right.

A tile should feel inevitable after reveal, not obvious before it.

## Meaning Inventory Gate

Writing begins only after the meaning inventory is approved.

Every meaning must:

- be a genuinely distinct dictionary sense;
- have a short editorial definition;
- include a source name and source URL;
- be fair, recognizable, playable, and meaningfully different;
- avoid dusty specialist trivia and predictable grammatical variations;
- be approved by a human before generation.

Ask whether a dictionary would likely give the sense its own numbered entry. Merge examples,
subtypes, grammatical variations, and predictable extensions.

Cover every strong playable meaning, but do not inflate a word with dictionary dust. The
approved ordinary meaning count determines `wordType`; legacy `wordType` values are not trusted.

## REAL Mask Standard

A REAL mask represents exactly one true meaning.

A REAL must be:

- legally true;
- fair and recognizable after reveal;
- compact and natural;
- a familiar human, cultural, legal, medical, sporting, historical, or everyday handle;
- indirect enough to create recognition without becoming a riddle.

A REAL must not be:

- a definition, synonym, explanation, or textbook label;
- a nearby object, result, association, or vibe pretending to be the meaning;
- a fake mini-scene or cute object acting;
- a duplicate of another meaning;
- a combination of multiple meanings;
- a phrase containing the headword or an obvious inflection.

Good angles include cultural handles, common phrases, pressure moments, familiar object
placements, and real-world labels. Use the angle people naturally carry in memory.

Examples:

- ADDRESS: `GPS DESTINATION`
- ADDRESS: `PRESIDENTIAL SPEECH`
- ARMS: `RIGHT TO BEAR THEM`
- BALANCE: `SCALES OF JUSTICE`
- BALL: `CINDERELLA'S MIDNIGHT DANCE`
- BELT: `ONE MORE NOTCH AFTER DINNER`

Avoid over-acted stage direction such as `GRIPS A PODIUM, CLEARS A THROAT` when the cleaner
cultural handle is `PRESIDENTIAL SPEECH`.

## Trap Standard

A trap is a guilty-close, legally wrong non-meaning.

Every trap must:

- bait one approved meaning direction;
- be close enough to cause hesitation;
- be wrong enough that a fair player cannot argue it is true;
- use the same compact human voice as REAL masks;
- include an editorial explanation of why it is tempting;
- use a recognized trap style.

Allowed trap styles:

- `neighbor`
- `scene`
- `tool`
- `result`
- `phrase`
- `sound-confusion`
- `almost-synonym`
- `sequence`
- `category`
- `family`

If a trap is actually true, promote it to a meaning when it is strong or remove it. Never leave
a secretly real tile marked false.

Useful review questions:

1. Is this a true meaning of the word?
2. Is this a common phrase using the word?
3. Is it merely a nearby object, action, tool, result, or family member?
4. Could a fair player reasonably defend it as correct?
5. Does it feel random or unnaturally written?

## Hidden Meaning Standard

Hidden meanings are a boss/reward layer, not a dumping ground.

- Normal words do not hide strong ordinary meanings.
- Strong playable meanings belong in the ordinary REAL layer.
- Boss-hidden meanings must be surprising, fair, source-supported, and recognizable after
  reveal.
- A boss-hidden meaning is excluded from ordinary visible meaning counts and the visible Boss
  tile budget.

## Length, Casing, and Voice

- Store player-facing tile text in uppercase.
- Prefer 2-6 words.
- Seven or eight words require review.
- Eight words is the hard maximum.
- Clarity beats padding; never add filler merely to hit a target length.
- Clean punctuation is allowed when it serves a familiar phrase.
- Avoid fake-poetic object acting and language nobody naturally uses.
- Avoid repeating key nouns, giveaway roots, rooms, or semantic scenery across one word's bank.
- REAL masks and traps must have similar length, specificity, and tone so wording never reveals
  truth before commitment.

## Snap Ladder

A strong word should move from recognition into reinterpretation:

1. familiar meaning;
2. nearby second meaning;
3. pivot meaning;
4. rare-but-fair discovery when appropriate.

Every approved word should contain at least one meaningful context switch. If it has only
obvious meanings and no pivot, it is a weak POLYWORDS candidate.

## Human Approval Checklist

Before approving a word, confirm:

1. Every REAL is legally true.
2. Every meaning family is distinct.
3. Every trap is legally wrong and guilty-close.
4. Every tile sounds human.
5. No tile leaks the headword.
6. No tile relies on fake object acting.
7. No wording pattern reveals REAL versus trap.
8. At least one meaning creates a Semantic Snap.
9. Sources were checked, especially for rare or hidden meanings.
10. A player would blame the rushed judgment, not the writing.

Fairness beats cleverness. Clean clever is welcome only after semantic legality is secure.
