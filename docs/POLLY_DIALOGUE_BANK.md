# POLYWORDS Polly Dialogue Bank

Purpose:
This document stores Polly dialogue ideas and ghost/system copy seeds.
Not every line here is approved for in-game use.
Use this as a content bank before future dialogue patches.

## Polly Voice Identity

Polly is a parrot mimic and polysemous word thief.
She steals meanings by mimicking human words.
She is a smug opponent, not a friendly helper.
She is a hoarder of stolen meanings and a taunting language burglar.

Polly should:
- sound smug, clever, thief-like, and theatrical
- use double-meaning/polysemous taunts when natural
- target the swipe, word, meaning, or theft
- stay short and mobile-readable
- feel funny and sharp, not cruel

Polly should not:
- sound warmly supportive
- explain mechanics like a tutorial
- directly insult the player's intelligence
- overuse puns
- use BINGO BANGO ZZZZINGO as her own line

BINGO BANGO ZZZZINGO is a rare game/system stinger only.

## Trap / Wrong Swipe Seeds

Approved or strong candidates:
- Gotcha.
- OOF.
- Almost doesn't count.
- That was MEANING to happen.
- That one almost belonged.
- Close enough to fool you.
- My trap had more bite.
- That swipe was butter-knife sharp.

Notes:
- "Almost doesn't count." is strong because traps are almost-meanings.
- "OOF." is best as quick impact bark.
- "Gotcha." is clean and smug, but common, so rotate with stronger POLYWORDS lines.
- "That was MEANING to happen." is strong Polly wordplay.

## Lines To Avoid Or Use Carefully

Avoid:
- A butter knife is sharper than you.

Reason:
It insults the player directly. Use:
- That swipe was butter-knife sharp.
instead.

Use carefully:
- Try again, featherbrain.

Reason:
Funny, but may be too mean if overused.

Avoid friendly/supportive Polly lines:
- Nice job.
- Great work.
- You got this.
- That's what I'm talking about.
- Word up.

## Future Patch Notes

Future dialogue patches should:
- keep Polly's active lines short
- preserve pop-in timing and budget
- avoid changing gameplay logic
- avoid turning system/UI copy into Polly dialogue
- prefer word-specific taunts only when existing systems support them cleanly
