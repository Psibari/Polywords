Lost my progress?

Progress is saved only on your phone. Deleting the app or using Reset Progress removes it, and it can't be recovered.

Daily reminder not showing?

Turn on Daily Reminder in Settings, then make sure notifications for POLYWORDS are allowed in your phone's settings.
