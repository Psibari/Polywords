Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Short version: POLYWORDS does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your phone.

What the game stores

Your game progress is saved on your device only: mastered words, Hunt stats, Daily Challenge history, the name you choose in Settings, and Polly's memory of your runs. None of it is sent to a server, and none of it is shared with or sold to anyone.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The developer receives no information about you or how you play.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, or use Reset Progress in Settings, and everything the game stored is gone for good.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a result, your phone's own share menu opens with a short text summary. Nothing is sent unless you choose where it goes.

Playtest data

During testing, the game keeps a local tally of run outcomes (survived, mastered, or lost, and at what round). It stays on your device. It only leaves if you tap Share Debug Stats yourself, and you can clear it at any time in Settings.

Daily reminder

The Daily Reminder is off by default. If you turn it on, the game asks your phone's permission to show one local notification when you haven't played the Daily in a while. It's set entirely on your device and uses no server.

Children

POLYWORDS does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change with it.
