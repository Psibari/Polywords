## 1. Wrong facts (fix whatever else you decide)
- **RANK** `rank_t0` "PURPLE HEART, EARNED FOR BRAVERY IN COMBAT" — the Purple Heart is for being wounded or killed, not for bravery.
- **CIRCUIT** `circuit_t2` "THE ONLY DIRECTION NASCAR DRIVERS TURN" — false. NASCAR Cup races road and street courses with right turns (Sonoma, Watkins Glen, COTA, Chicago, San Diego). `left_r9` says "famous for", which is right.
- **HORN** `horn_r2` (a REAL) "YOU CAN'T PLAY THE BLUES WITHOUT IT" — false. Plenty of blues is guitar and harmonica, no horn.
- **BOW** `bow_t3` "WHERE YOU CAN READ THE NAME OF A SHIP" — the ship's name is on the bow too. Coast Guard rules put it on both sides of the bow and the stern. A player who says BOW is right and gets marked wrong.
- **BILL** `bill_t2` "GAVEL SIGNS IT INTO LAW" — the thing signed into law IS a bill. And a gavel does not sign anything.
- **BATTERY** hidden `battery_h02` REAL "THE NEIGHBORHOOD WHERE THE CHARGING BULL LIVES" — the Charging Bull stands at Bowling Green in the Financial District, next to the Battery, not in it. The Battery is a park, not a neighborhood.
- **CHECK** hidden `check_h01` REAL "SEEN ON GYM SHORTS BUT NOT ON A SUIT JACKET" — checked patterns are classic on suit jackets, so the clue says the opposite of the truth. A swoosh is not a check.
- **CONTEST** `contest_r03` (a REAL) "WHAT A BASEBALL GAME WASN’T AFTER A SHUTOUT" — a shutout can be a 1-0 nail-biter. "No contest" means one-sided, not shutout.
- **BRIDGE** `bridge_r3` (a REAL) "THE RAPPER’S PART OF A DANCE SONG" — a bridge is the linking section of a song (dictionary), not "the rapper's part".
- Small ones: `rank_t1` "CASTLING SWAPS THE KING AND ROOK'S POSITIONS" (castling does not swap them: the king moves two squares and the rook hops over). `court_r0` "TRAFFIC, CIVIL, CRIMINAL, FEDERAL, THEN SUPREME" (traffic, civil and criminal are not steps on a ladder below federal).

## 2. Traps that are really true (strong)
A fair player can claim these and be right. Worst words first.

**DRIVE** — five of ten traps are the motivation sense, the same as REAL `drive_r1`. A dog's urge to chase is literally called "prey drive".
- `drive_t1` "WHAT MAKES A DOG CHASE THE BALL BEFORE YOU EVEN THROW IT"
- `drive_t2` "THE URGE THAT MAKES YOU CHECK YOUR PHONE AGAIN FIVE SECONDS LATER"
- `drive_t3` "WHAT KEEPS YOU GOING AFTER EVERYONE ELSE HAS QUIT"
- `drive_t5` "THE NEED THAT MAKES SOMEONE HAVE TO WIN AT EVERYTHING"
- `drive_t6` "WHAT PUSHES A STUDENT TO STUDY ALL NIGHT BEFORE THE FINAL"

**TRAIN** — all three traps.
- `train_t0` "SUBWAY DOORS CLOSE IN YOUR FACE" — a subway is a train.
- `train_t2` "LACE HEM DRAGGING DOWN THE AISLE" — that IS a dress train (`train_r2`).
- `train_t1` "COACH BLOWS THE WHISTLE TO START DRILLS" — practice drills are training (`train_r1`).

**TRIP** — all three traps.
- `trip_t0` "TWO WEEKS SIPPING DRINKS ON A CRUISE" — a cruise is a trip.
- `trip_t2` "SEEING COLORS THAT AREN'T REALLY THERE" — that is tripping (`trip_r2`).
- `trip_t1` "BANANA PEEL SENDS YOU FLYING" — a fall, same as `trip_r1`.

**CORD** (a confidence/easy word, so early in a hunt) — twine, string and drawstrings are cord. Sweatpants strings are sold as "drawcords". No naming frame protects these.
- `cord_t03` "WHAT BINDS A STACK OF NEWSPAPERS TOGETHER"
- `cord_t04` "WHAT YOU TIE TO KEEP SWEATPANTS FROM FALLING"
- `cord_t05` "WHAT YOU WRAP AROUND A PACKAGE BEFORE MAILING"

**FACE**
- `face_t00` "A BUILDING’S PUBLIC FRONT" — dictionary: "the face of a building", synonym facade.
- `face_t01` "THE VISIBLE SIDE OF THE MOON" — "the face of the moon".
- `face_t03` "THE SIDE TURNED TOWARD THE SUNSET" — the west face (same sense as `face_r06`).

**FIGURE**
- `figure_t01` "YOUR BEST GUESS WITHOUT DOING THE MATH" — a "ballpark figure", "I figure".
- `figure_t06` "DECIMAL DIGITS ON A PRICE TAG" — digits are figures (`figure_r00` is "six figures").
- `figure_t04` "THE RESULT AFTER PRESSING EQUALS" — the result is a figure.

**FINE**
- `fine_t02` "THE WORD FOR SOMETHING THAT PASSES INSPECTION" — fine = acceptable (`fine_r00`).
- `fine_t04` "THE RESULT WHEN AN INSPECTION FINDS NO PROBLEMS" — "everything's fine".

**STICK**
- `stick_t1` "SUPERGLUE BONDS IN SECONDS" — that is sticking (`stick_r1`).
- `stick_t2` "RELAY BATON PASSED HAND TO HAND" — a baton is a stick.
- `stick_t3` "CLUTCH POPPED TOO FAST, ENGINE STALLS" — popping the clutch is a stick-shift moment (`stick_r4`).

**POOL**
- `pool_t2` "SPLITTING THE BILL EVENLY FOUR WAYS" — everyone chipping in is pooling (`pool_r2`).
- `pool_t3` "SHORTLIST OF NAMES FOR THE JOB" — a shortlist is a candidate pool (`pool_r4`). The difference is too fine to be fair.
